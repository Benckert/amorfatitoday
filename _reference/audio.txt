Optional. Put an ambient clip here as:

  ambient.mp3

A minute or two, quiet, loopable — a held note, room tone, a fragment
of the piece. It loops seamlessly and fades in over two seconds.

It never plays on its own: there is a small "sound" toggle in the
bottom-left corner, off by default.

If this file does not exist, the toggle hides itself and the page is
simply silent. Nothing breaks.

--- note, September 2026 ---
The feature was removed. The audio/ folder held only this note, the
clip was never made, and the page requested audio/ambient.mp3 on every
visit to discover that — one 404 per visitor for a button that never
appeared.

What went with it: the <button class="sound"> markup and its <audio>
element, the .sound CSS block, the forty lines of script that faded it
in and out, and fonts/cormgar-300-latin.woff2 and its -ext pair, which
nothing else on the page set.

Bringing it back is a revert of the commit that removed it, plus the
clip at audio/ambient.mp3. The description above still applies.
